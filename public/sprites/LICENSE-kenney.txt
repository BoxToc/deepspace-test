Sprites in this folder come from Kenney's Isometric Buildings, Isometric City and
Isometric Landscape packs (www.kenney.nl), renamed for readability.

Exception: power-station-1.png, power-station-2.png and power-station-3.png are
original DreamGrid art, rendered in the Kenney style by tools/sprite-pipeline
from geometric models (tools/sprite-pipeline/models/power-station.js). They
are not part of Kenney's packs.
